Step 05
⚡
Human judgment required here: The output framework is a starting point, not a final structure. The researcher must adapt it based on actual findings — research rarely goes where you design it to go. The analysis and synthesis sections must be written by a human researcher. AI can draft, structure, and refine — it cannot produce original insight or field-grounded interpretation.
Workflow Summary
What this workflow demonstrates: AI compressed what would typically take 3–5 days of desk research into a structured, reviewable framework in under 20 minutes. The researcher's contribution — the research question, the initial hypothesis, the methodological judgment, and the final analysis — remains irreducibly human. This is the correct role for AI in development analytics: compressor of research time, not replacer of research thinking.
